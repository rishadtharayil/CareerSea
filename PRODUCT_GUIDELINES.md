# 🚢 Product Guidelines & Vision

This document defines the user experience, monetization strategy, and upcoming feature roadmap for CareerSea.

## 🌟 Vision: From Generator to Navigator
CareerSea is evolving from a simple "Roadmap Generator" into a comprehensive "Career Discovery Engine." 

### Shift 1: Discovery over Certainty
- **Old way:** Asking "What is your dream job?".
- **New way:** Identifying user's natural problem-solving architecture and suggesting 3 unexpected paths.

### Shift 2: Interactive over Static
- **Old way:** A static list of steps.
- **New way:** A dashboard where users can check off completed steps and track their journey.

## 💰 Monetization Strategy (Phase 1-3)
1.  **Phase 1 (Ad-Supported):** Google AdSense integration at the footer of results.
2.  **Phase 2 (Affiliate):** Link to specific courses (Udemy/Coursera) in the "Resources" section.
3.  **Phase 3 (Premium):** "Deep-Dive" roadmaps with mentor-style AI feedback for a small one-time fee.

## 🗺️ Feature Roadmap

### High Priority
- [ ] **Multi-Path Suggestions:** AI should suggest 3 distinct career paths after the assessment.
- [ ] **"Explore" Directory:** A browsable list of careers with pre-generated roadmaps.
- [ ] **Diagnostic Assessment:** Rewrite questions to focus on strengths rather than pre-defined goals.

### Medium Priority
- [ ] **Step Tracking:** Allow users to mark roadmap steps as "Completed."
- [ ] **AI Career Coach:** A chat interface contextually aware of the user's roadmap.

### Low Priority
- [ ] **Resume Builder:** Auto-generate a resume based on the roadmap goal.
- [ ] **LinkedIn Integration:** Share achievement badges for completed roadmap steps.

## 🎨 UI/UX Philosophy
- **Neubrutalism:** Keep it raw, fast, and high-energy.
- **Micro-interactions:** Use Framer Motion to make every action feel "clickable" and satisfying.
- **Transparency:** Clearly state that suggestions are AI-generated but high-quality.
